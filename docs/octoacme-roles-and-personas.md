# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Technical Leads** on architectural guidance and code quality standards
- Collaborate with **QA Leads** to clarify acceptance criteria and ensure testability
- Receive priorities and requirements from **Product Managers**
- Report progress and blockers to **Project Managers** in standups
- Partner with **Business Analysts** to clarify requirements and edge cases

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Project Managers** on prioritization and timeline planning
- Work with **Business Analysts** to capture and refine requirements
- Align with **Executive Sponsors** on strategic priorities and trade-offs
- Gather feedback from **Stakeholder/Customer Representatives** on feature validation
- Guide **Developers** through acceptance criteria and product vision

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Facilitate collaboration between **Product Managers** and delivery team
- Work with **Scrum Masters** to ensure process adherence and team health
- Coordinate with **Technical Leads** on architecture and technical dependencies
- Escalate risks and blockers identified by **Developers** and **QA Leads**
- Report progress to **Executive Sponsors** and manage stakeholder expectations

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads ensure product quality, define testing strategy, and validate acceptance criteria. They collaborate with developers and product managers to establish quality standards and testing processes.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and acceptance criteria interpretation
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with release schedule
- Conduct manual QA and validate automated test coverage
- Report quality metrics and identify quality risks
- Collaborate on Definition of Done requirements
- Establish quality gates and release criteria

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Reduce cycle time for QA without compromising quality
- Establish trust in automated and manual testing
- Prevent production issues through early validation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and execution syncs
- Quality metrics in weekly status reports
- Test plan reviews with developers and product managers
- Defect triage and prioritization discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Developers** to clarify acceptance criteria and establish testable requirements
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on quality expectations and release readiness
- Coordinate with **Technical Leads** on testing architecture and automation strategy
- Provide quality metrics to **Project Managers** for risk assessment
- Validate solutions with **Stakeholder/Customer Representatives** during acceptance testing

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical guidance, make architectural decisions, and ensure technical feasibility. They mentor developers and manage technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Mentor and support development team members
- Ensure code quality and technical standards
- Participate in capacity planning and estimation
- Guide technology choices and integration strategies

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and prevent architectural risks
- Develop team technical skills
- Enable fast, reliable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture decision documents
- Risk register updates
- Code review participation
- Technical mentoring sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on architectural decisions and code quality standards
- Advise **Project Managers** on technical feasibility and effort estimation
- Collaborate with **QA Leads** on testing architecture and automation approaches
- Support **Product Managers** in understanding technical trade-offs
- Champion technical solutions with **Executive Sponsors** when needed

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather and document requirements, clarify business needs, and ensure alignment between customer expectations and technical delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business and user requirements
- Clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Facilitate stakeholder discussions and feedback loops
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Document use cases, user stories, and workflows
- Bridge communication between business and technical teams

### Goals
- Ensure customer needs are accurately captured and met
- Reduce scope misalignment and rework
- Improve clarity between business and technical teams
- Minimize requirement ambiguity

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops with stakeholders
- User story and acceptance criteria collaboration
- Validation discussions during sprint review
- Stakeholder feedback synthesis

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** to refine and prioritize requirements
- Work with **Developers** to clarify requirements and answer detailed questions
- Partner with **Stakeholder/Customer Representatives** to gather and validate business needs
- Support **Project Managers** in scope management and change requests
- Assist **QA Leads** in understanding user workflows and edge cases

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide budget approval, strategic alignment, and escalation authority. They ensure project alignment with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Provide strategic guidance and priority setting
- Escalate and resolve high-level blockers
- Monitor project health and business outcomes
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Remove organizational impediments

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers strategic business value
- Protect project resources and stakeholder alignment
- Reduce organizational friction and conflicting priorities
- Enable executive-level decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gate approvals (Initiation, Planning, Release)
- Ad-hoc escalation and issue resolution
- Executive reporting on outcomes and ROI

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Review progress and outcomes reported by **Project Managers**
- Provide strategic context and priorities to **Product Managers**
- Escalate organizational blockers raised by the team
- Approve resource trade-offs and timeline adjustments
- Represent project interests at executive level

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach the team on agile practices. They ensure team effectiveness and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove process obstacles and blockers
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Track team velocity and process metrics
- Support psychological safety and team dynamics
- Help teams optimize their workflow

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and throughput
- Foster continuous improvement and learning
- Maintain team morale and psychological safety
- Enable self-organizing teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective action item tracking
- Metrics and process dashboards
- One-on-one coaching conversations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Support **Project Managers** in sprint execution and planning
- Coach **Developers** on agile practices and sustainable pace
- Help **QA Leads** integrate testing into sprint workflow
- Partner with **Technical Leads** on technical debt management
- Facilitate collaboration across all roles in agile ceremonies

---

## Stakeholder / Customer Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent customer needs, validate solutions, and provide feedback. They ensure product decisions are grounded in customer reality.

### Responsibilities
- Provide customer perspective on requirements and priorities
- Validate solutions against customer needs
- Participate in reviews and feedback sessions
- Report customer satisfaction and usage metrics
- Champion customer voice in trade-off discussions
- Advocate for customer usability and user experience

### Goals
- Ensure product-market fit and customer satisfaction
- Reduce disconnect between product team and customers
- Inform data-driven product decisions
- Drive customer adoption and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Requirements validation sessions
- Sprint reviews and demos
- Customer feedback and usage metrics sharing
- Escalation of customer-impacting issues
- User testing and feedback sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Product Managers** to validate priorities and define success metrics
- Provide feedback to **Developers** during sprint reviews and demos
- Collaborate with **Business Analysts** to gather and validate requirements
- Share customer perspectives with **Project Managers** for risk assessment
- Ensure solutions meet customer needs validated by **QA Leads**

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
